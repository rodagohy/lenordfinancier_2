Our services

Seven ways to support you

From analyzing your situation to protecting your loved ones, every service is tailored to your needs and your budget.

Personal Financial Analysis

A detailed review of your income, debts, protections and goals to build a customized strategy.

  • Overview of your income, expenses and debts
  • Review of your current coverage
  • Short, medium and long-term goals
  • Clear strategy and personalized follow-up

Life, Disability and Critical Illness Insurance

Tailored coverage to secure your future and that of your loved ones.

  • Term or permanent life insurance
  • Income protection in case of disability
  • Lump-sum benefit for critical illness
  • Coverage adjusted to your budget

Health, Dental and Travel Insurance

Flexible solutions to protect your health, at home and abroad.

  • Health care and prescription drugs
  • Dental care
  • Travel insurance and emergency care abroad
  • Options for students and newcomers

Savings and Investments

Personalized strategies to grow your wealth with the right tools.

  • TFSA: Tax-Free Savings Account
  • FHSA: saving for your first home
  • RRSP: Registered Retirement Savings Plan
  • RESP: saving for your children’s education

Retirement Planning and Estate Management

Prepare for a stable future and safeguard the legacy you wish to pass on.

  • Planning your retirement income
  • Protecting your assets
  • Passing on to your loved ones
  • Regular review of your plan

Home and Auto Insurance

Competitive offers for comprehensive and affordable protection.

  • Protection for your home and belongings
  • Coverage for your vehicle
  • Offers compared for your budget

Support for Newcomers and International Students

Our dearest mission: helping you understand Canada’s financial system, build a strong foundation and reach your goals faster.

  • Understand the Canadian financial system
  • Build a strong foundation from day one
  • Protect your health and your loved ones
  • Build a network and move forward with confidence
