About

A journey dedicated to your future

Le Nord Financier supports individuals, families and newcomers in managing their personal finances, with a human and personalized approach.

Let’s get acquainted
Our mission

Making financial planning accessible, understandable and tailored to each life situation.

We believe that good financial guidance is built on trust, clarity and simplicity. Through a human and personalized approach, we help our clients build strong foundations to reach their goals — whether it’s protecting their family, saving for the future or investing with confidence.

Guided by our values, we make every meeting an opportunity to learn, understand and take action. In Quebec and Ontario, in person or by video call, we move forward at your pace.

Her dearest mission

To welcome international students and newcomers, help them understand the Canadian financial system, build strong networks and lay the foundation for lasting financial security.

Background

From the aircraft cabin to financial advice

Founded by Adjoua Laetitia Konan, Le Nord Financier reflects a journey marked by excellence, discipline and a passion for service.

  1. More than ten years

    Chief Flight Attendant

    A decade looking after passengers and leading the cabin crew, with rigour and a strong sense of service.

  2. A turning point

    iA Financial Group

    She put her human and organizational skills at the service of the financial industry, and quickly stood out for her attentive listening, analytical ability and professionalism.

  3. Awards

    1st Challenge Winner

    Also recognized in the President’s Campaign — two distinctions that reflect her commitment to excellence.

  4. Today

    Founder of Le Nord Financier

    A financial security advisor in Quebec and Ontario, she supports families, professionals and newcomers.

  5. Education

    DEC in Commerce and Finance

    She is currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Commerce, combining knowledge, experience and empathy.
