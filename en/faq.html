Our services

How can Le Nord Financier help me manage my finances?

We provide personalized guidance to help you save smarter, plan ahead and make confident investment decisions.

What services do you offer?

Personal financial analysis; life, disability and critical illness insurance; health, dental and travel insurance; savings and investments (TFSA, FHSA, RRSP, RESP); retirement planning and estate management; home and auto insurance; support for newcomers and international students.

Are your financial plans personalized?

Always. Every plan is built after a thorough analysis of your situation, risk tolerance, timeline and goals.

Do I need an advisor if I already use a budgeting app?

Yes. Apps track your spending; an advisor helps you interpret your numbers, build a strategy and make decisions that align with your long-term goals.

Appointments and follow-up

How can I book an appointment?

Book a first consultation online, call us at +1 (613) 262-4360 or write to us. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Do you meet in person or remotely?

Both: we can meet in person or by video call, whichever you prefer.

How quickly can we start working together?

After our first meeting, we typically begin building your strategy within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the complexity of your situation.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Every meeting is confidential, and your personal information is used only to advise you and respond to your requests.

Where do you practise?

Newcomers

Do you support international students and newcomers?

It is our dearest mission: helping you understand the Canadian financial system, build a strong network and lay the foundation for lasting financial security.

I just arrived in Canada. Where should I start?

With a first meeting: we review your situation, your coverage and your goals, then set the priorities for your first months together.
